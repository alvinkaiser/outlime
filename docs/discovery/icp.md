# ICP — Restaurants (Phase 0 Discovery)

Source: PRD v0.1 AI Agents for Online Businesses. Vertical fixed: Restaurants.

## Target profile
- Independent restaurants, 1-3 branches, Nairobi + environs
- 10-50 delivery/takeaway orders/day plus dine-in inquiries
- Order intake: WhatsApp first, Instagram DMs second, calls/walk-ins rest
- Menu shared as PDF/image, prices change occasionally, delivery zones/fees informal
- Payments: M-Pesa Till or Paybill, owner confirms via SMS/Till app
- Backend: no POS / spreadsheet / WooCommerce / simple POS (to be validated — PRD Open Question)
- Owner or 1 staff member answers chats, including late/night

## Out of scope for discovery
- Large chains with integrated POS + riders
- Businesses with no WhatsApp ordering

## Screener (2 min)
1. Orders/week via WhatsApp/IG?
2. Who replies? What hours?
3. How do you take payment and confirm it?
4. How often does menu/price change?
5. Willing to do 30 min interview + share 10 anonymized chats?

Qualify if: >=30 WhatsApp orders/week, owner-in-chat, M-Pesa, willing to pilot.

## Outreach goal
- List 20, interview 5-10 owners
- Capture: volume, tools, time spent, payment errors, language samples
